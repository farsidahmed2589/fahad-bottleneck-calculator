    Bottleneck calculator FAQ

    What is a bottleneck?

    A bottleneck means one part, usually the CPU or the GPU, limits how many frames per second the whole PC can produce. The other part waits for it.

    Is a GPU running at 100% bad?

    No. In most games at higher resolutions and settings the GPU should be the limiting part. A fully used GPU means you are getting everything it can give.

    Does more VRAM give more FPS?

    Not by itself. Extra VRAM only helps when a game needs more memory than your card has, and then it removes stutter. Memory type and speed (bandwidth) affect speed more than capacity does.

    Why does a fast GPU show a CPU bottleneck at low resolution?

    At low resolutions the GPU finishes each frame quickly and waits for the CPU. Raising resolution or quality moves the limit back to the GPU.

    How accurate is this calculator?

    It is an estimate, not a measurement. CPU and GPU scores are approximate and about 20% uncertain, so results are shown with a confidence level and a range. Real FPS depends on the game, scene, drivers, cooling and laptop power limits. GTA VI numbers are predictions because no PC version has been announced.
